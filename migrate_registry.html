🔄 Migrate Teams to Registry

This tool reads all teams from your existing Jalala Zone tournaments and saves them to the Teams Registry so they appear in the public Teams tab and the admin "Pick from Registry" dropdown.

ℹ️ What this does
Safe to run multiple times — it won't create duplicates. Teams already in the registry are skipped.

It copies: Team name, Coach name
It does NOT change: Any match results, points, standings, or tournament data.
